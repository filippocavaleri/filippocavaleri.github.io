Research

Working Papers

  • Fiscal Insurance and the Pricing of Government Debt

    2026 The WFA Brattle Group PhD Candidate Awards for Outstanding Research
    Draft
    I examine how government debt impacts bond pricing when fiscal policy intersects with uninsurable risks. Debt issuance erodes fiscal insurance and hurts welfare beyond a debt threshold. Debt thresholds vary with foreign demand levels and tax progressivity. Ricardian neutrality applies in incomplete markets with lump-sum taxation.

  • A Preferred-Habitat Model with a Corporate Sector

    2024 The WFA Brattle Group PhD Candidate Awards for Outstanding Research 2024 NFA Best PhD Student Paper Award 2023 Yiran Fan Memorial Fellowship
    Draft SSRN
    I study the interactions between interest rate risk, credit risk, and bond quantities in Treasury and corporate bond yields. A decline in Treasury supply can adversely impact corporate yields by raising the price of credit risk.

  • The Demand for Safe Assets

    With Angelo Ranaldo and Enzo Rossi

    2025 Arnold Zellner Doctoral Prize
    Draft SSRN SFI SNB
    We analyze how investment horizon heterogeneity shapes safe asset demand and auction bidding, using Swiss Treasury auction data with granular bidder-level information.

  • The Convenience Yield and the Demand for U.S. Treasury Securities

    Liew Fama-Miller Fellowship for Best 2nd Year Paper
    Draft SSRN
    I investigate heterogeneous investor preferences for non-pecuniary Treasury attributes. Depository institutions and brokers value Treasury convenience, while households and pension funds find it less attractive.
