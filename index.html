I am an Assistant Professor of Finance at the Rotman School of Management at the University of Toronto.

I have received my PhD in financial economics from the University of Chicago, Booth School of Business and Kenneth C. Griffin Department of Economics.

I study how government debt, taxation, and financial markets interact in interesting ways.

Research Interests

  • Government debt and asset prices
  • Safe assets
  • Term structure and fixed income
  • Macro-finance
