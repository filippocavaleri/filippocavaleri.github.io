# Project: Academic Website Redesign

## Goal

Convert Filippo Cavaleri's GitHub Pages Jekyll site (https://filippocavaleri.github.io/) into a self-contained, locally-served static website with a significantly improved visual design. The site should retain the same content structure and navigation, but look much better than the generic AcademicPages/Minimal Mistakes theme.

## Current Site

- **Source**: https://github.com/filippocavaleri/filippocavaleri.github.io
- **Theme**: AcademicPages (fork of Minimal Mistakes Jekyll theme)
- **Pages**: Home, Research, Teaching, Discussions, CV

### Page Content Summary

**Home**: Headshot, name, title (PhD Student in Financial Economics, University of Chicago), short bio ("I study how government debt, taxation, and financial markets interact in interesting ways."), research interests, contact links (email, Google Scholar, SSRN, LinkedIn, GitHub), job market notice.

**Research**: Three sections:
- *Job Market Paper*: "Fiscal Insurance and the Pricing of Government Debt"
- *Working Papers*: "A Preferred-Habitat Model with a Corporate Sector" (WFA PhD Candidate Award, 2024 NFA Best PhD Student Paper, Yiran Fan Memorial Fellowship), "The Convenience Yield and the Demand for U.S. Treasury Securities" (Liew Fama-Miller Fellowship), "The Demand for Safe Assets" (with Ranaldo & Rossi; 2025 Arnold Zellner Doctoral Prize)
- *Policy Papers*: "Swiss Treasury Bond Auctions: An Update" (with Gortan, Ranaldo, Rossi; SNB)

**Teaching**: TA roles at U of C (PhD and MBA courses) and U of St. Gallen. Working groups organized (Theory Reading Group, Asset Pricing Working Group, Finance Brownbag).

**Discussions**: Three conference discussions in 2025 (EFA, MFA, SGE) with slides.

**CV**: Downloadable PDF.

## Design Direction

- Same five-page structure and content organization
- Significantly better visual design: clean, modern, elegant — appropriate for an academic on the job market
- Self-contained: no Jekyll/Ruby dependency, pure HTML/CSS/JS
- Locally served (no GitHub Pages build pipeline)
- Full control over typography, spacing, layout, colors
